VaultMesh is an open source distributed storage system designed to provide excellent performance, reliability and scalability. Engineered with self-healing Galois field mathematical repair, cryptographically verified Merkle trees, and linearizable Raft consensus, VaultMesh delivers multi-petabyte durability with zero centralized bottlenecks.
